A small-batch studio

Frosted Frameworks is a one-person studio today, built to grow into a small team. Every project gets my direct attention, because I’m the one doing the work.

Hi, I’m Marjorie Pare, the founder of Frosted Frameworks. I’ve spent more than ten years in Unity game development, on games like Fight Knight, Devil Engine: Ignition, Bug Fables, and Star Garden, with roles across programming, 3D modeling, and testing. Ever since I was young, games have felt like the perfect medium for creating powerful, emotional experiences.

For the past two years, most of my work has gone into Cyber-PARK, a web platform that teaches cybersecurity in K–12 classrooms. Building educational experiences like this is the work I enjoy most.

I’ve also spent six years decorating cakes. I love every part of creative development, from 3D modeling to programming to UI design, and it’s a lot like baking and decorating: many different skills working together to make one thing people enjoy. I started Frosted Frameworks LLC to bring that range to a wider set of clients.

The house recipe

After years of both game development and cake decorating, I’ve found that good software and good baking have a lot in common: solid fundamentals underneath, something delightful on top, and patience in between.

That’s the approach behind every project here. Build it properly, make it a pleasure to use, and check in often enough that nothing comes out of the oven as a surprise.

// Serves: every client, every project

  bakeTime: "Short, honest iterations",
};
