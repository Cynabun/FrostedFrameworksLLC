How a project comes together

Every project follows the same simple recipe, whether it’s a weekend prototype or a months-long build.

  1. Taste

    An intro call to understand what you’re making and what’s standing in the way.

  2. Recipe

    A written scope with goals, milestones, and a clear estimate before any work starts.

  3. Bake

    Short iterations, with something you can see and try at every check-in.

  4. Serve

    Launch, handoff, and follow-up support so the work keeps holding up.
